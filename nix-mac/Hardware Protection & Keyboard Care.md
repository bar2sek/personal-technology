# ⌨️ Hardware Protection & Keyboard Care

A complete setup and step-by-step application guide for protecting a brand-new MacBook keyboard from keycap shine, finger oils, and screen contact wear over time.

## 2. 🧰 Tools Checklist

- [ ] **Fine-tip curved tweezers:** Prevents skin oils from touching the decal's adhesive underside during placement.
- [ ] **70% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA):** 
  > [!WARNING]
  > Do **not** use 90%+ IPA (can degrade certain keycap coatings) or window cleaners/soapy water (leaves residues).
- [ ] **Lint-free microfiber cloth:** For the prep wipe-down.
- [ ] **Low-tack painter’s tape or dust stickers:** For picking up micro-lint right before sticker placement.
- [ ] **Good direct desk lighting:** Critical for seeing alignment against the keycap edges.

---

## 3. 🛠️ Step-by-Step Installation Walkthrough

### Step 1: Environmental Prep & Device Setup
* **Reduce airborne dust:** Work in a clean, low-airflow room (avoid running ceiling fans or open windows).
* **Wash hands thoroughly:** Use degreasing hand soap to eliminate skin oils.
* **Power down the Mac:**
  * Shut down completely via Apple Menu ($\blackapple$) > **Shut Down**.
  * *Alternative:* If you prefer keeping the keyboard backlighting illuminated as an alignment guide, install a lightweight utility like `KeyboardCleanTool` or `LockKeyboard` so phantom keypresses don't disrupt your system.

---

### Step 2: Surface Decontamination (Even on Brand-New Units)
1. Lightly mist or dampen your microfiber cloth with **70% Isopropyl Alcohol**.
   > [!CAUTION]
   > Never spray liquid directly onto the keyboard deck or keycaps.
2. Gently wipe each keycap to strip away factory mold-release agents and packaging residues.
3. Wait **30–60 seconds** for the alcohol to flash off and dry completely.

---

### Step 3: Dust Removal
1. Take a small strip of blue painter’s tape (or the dust sticker provided in your decal kit).
2. Lightly dab each keycap to pull off any microscopic lint or hair before placing a decal.

---

### Step 4: Peeling & Tweezers Alignment
1. **Peel from backing:** Using the fine-point tweezers, gently catch the very outer corner of a key decal and peel it off the backing sheet.
2. **Align top-to-bottom:** Hover the sticker directly over the keycap.
   * Align the sticker with the top-left corner and the legend cutout/print.
   * Ensure there is an even, hairline gap around all four outer edges of the keycap.
3. **Drop into place:** Touch down one edge first, then let the rest of the film settle naturally.
   > [!TIP]
   > If it lands crooked, use the tweezers to lift it straight up gently and re-align—avoid stretching the film.

---

### Step 5: Burnishing & Seating the Adhesive
1. Once aligned, place the dry microfiber cloth over the keycap.
2. Press down firmly with the pad of your thumb, sweeping from the center outward to push out any micro-air bubbles and activate the pressure-sensitive adhesive.
3. Pay extra attention to the four corners to make sure they are seated flat.

---

## 4. 🛡️ Post-Application & Long-Term Care

* **Curing period:** Avoid wiping or aggressively rubbing the keyboard for the first **12–24 hours** to give the low-tack adhesive full contact time.
* **Routine cleaning:** Every 1–2 weeks, wipe down the decal-covered keys with a lightly dampened microfiber cloth (water or 70% IPA) to remove superficial oils.
* **Screen protection in transit:** Place your ultra-thin microfiber buffer cloth (e.g., **UPPERCASE GhostBlanket**) flat across the keyboard deck before closing the lid into a backpack or tight sleeve to eliminate keyboard-to-glass imprint risks.
